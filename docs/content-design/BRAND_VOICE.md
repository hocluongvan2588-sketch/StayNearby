# Brand Voice & Positioning

## 1. Brand Positioning

**VietNomad Stays** helps people find comfortable weekly and monthly stays across Vietnam, explore neighborhoods, and experience local life at their own pace.

VietNomad is built around four practical pillars:
- **Stay:** Find serviced apartments, villas, coliving spaces, boutique hotels, and lodges suited for multi-week stays.
- **Work:** Access reliable Wi-Fi and dedicated desk setups when you need them.
- **Live:** Settle into everyday life with kitchens, laundry, and clear neighborhood context.
- **Explore:** Discover nearby restaurants, cafes, landmarks, wellness spots, and local partners.

### Who We Serve
Our guests include long-stay travelers, couples, families, business travelers, relocating professionals, and remote workers.

**Positioning rule:** Never frame VietNomad as a platform exclusively for "Digital Nomads" or "Remote Workers." Avoid overusing *Digital Nomad*, *Remote Work*, *Workation*, *Productivity*, or *Work-Ready* unless describing an optional filter, verification badge, or workspace amenity. Guests should never feel they need to work remotely to belong on VietNomad.

## 2. Core Brand Voice Traits

| Trait | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Human** | Write like a helpful person talking directly to the user, not a template or marketing machine. |
| **Welcoming** | Open and approachable, without forced enthusiasm or exclamation marks. |
| **Clear** | Lead with the point. Tell users what something costs, how it works, or what happens next. |
| **Practical** | Focus on real facts: check-in payment split, Wi-Fi speed, kitchen type, cancellation policy, neighborhood location. |
| **Trustworthy** | Be honest and transparent. Never invent guarantees, safety claims, or verification badges without underlying data. |
| **International** | Use natural, plain international English accessible to travelers from any country. |
| **Locally connected** | Show genuine familiarity with Vietnamese cities and neighborhoods where it helps the user decide. |

## 3. Tone of Voice by System

### Guest Platform (`/`, `/stays`, `/stays/[slug]`, `/destinations`, `/guides`, `/dashboard`, `/deals`, `/community`)
- **Tone:** Friendly, clear, helpful, grounded in discovery.
- **Goal:** Help guests compare stays, understand total cost and check-in terms, explore neighborhoods, book smoothly, and manage trips or support requests.
- **Examples:**
  - *Where would you like to stay?*
  - *Find your next stay.*
  - *Explore nearby.*
  - *Your upcoming stays*
  - *Check availability*
  - *Save stay*
  - *Contact host*

### Host Console (`/host/**`, `/list-your-stay`)
- **Tone:** Practical, direct, action-oriented, supportive.
- **Goal:** Give hosts clarity on arrivals, calendar availability, nightly/package pricing, direct-at-check-in payouts, and guest messages.
- **Examples:**
  - *Manage your listings.*
  - *Update nightly rates.*
  - *You have 3 upcoming arrivals.*
  - *Your listing is under review.*
  - *Confirm check-in and rent received.*
  - *Payout scheduled.*
- **Avoid:** Inspirational slogans, promotional banners, or fluffy onboarding promises inside host operational screens.

### Admin BOS (`/admin/**`)
- **Tone:** Neutral, concise, precise, operational.
- **Goal:** Support internal workflows across CRM, listing moderation, bookings, finance, refunds, and customer support with zero ambiguity.
- **Examples:**
  - *Pending approvals*
  - *Host acquisition pipeline*
  - *Open support tickets*
  - *Refund requested*
  - *Payment received*
  - *Review application*
- **Avoid:** Marketing language, emotional phrasing, or unnecessary explanatory paragraphs on internal queues.
